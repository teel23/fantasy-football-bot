Feature Engine v1.1 (Fully Automated Design)
We’re going to slightly refine the 10 features so they require zero manual input.
We’ll also define how each one is computed in a way you can actually code.

1️⃣ Base Projection Score (Automated)
Input: Weekly projection Normalization:


projection_score = player_projection / max_projection_at_position

Range: 0–1

2️⃣ Recent Trend Score
Instead of “feel,” we use weighted momentum.


trend_score =
(0.5 × last_game_pts
 + 0.3 × game_2_pts
 + 0.2 × game_3_pts)
÷ positional_average

Then normalize 0–1.
This makes it responsive but not overreactive.

3️⃣ Usage Stability Score
We turn usage into a composite metric:


usage_score =
(0.4 × snap%)
+ (0.4 × opportunity_share)
+ (0.2 × route_participation%)

Normalize against positional max.
This becomes your role anchor.

4️⃣ Opportunity Share


opportunity_share =
(player_targets + player_carries)
÷ team_total_opportunities

This is already a percentage → normalize 0–1.

5️⃣ Matchup Difficulty Index
Use fantasy points allowed vs position.


matchup_score =
league_avg_allowed ÷ defense_allowed

Bad defense = higher score Good defense = lower score
Then normalize across that week’s matchups.

6️⃣ Game Environment Score


game_score =
(vegas_total / highest_week_total)
× (1 - |spread| / max_spread)

Close game + high total = high score.

7️⃣ Volatility Index (Fully Automated)


volatility =
std_dev(last_5_games)
÷ mean(last_5_games)

Higher value = more boom/bust.
This does NOT increase player score. It lowers confidence later.

8️⃣ Role Risk Score
Instead of manual:


role_risk =
1 - (snap% variance last 4 games)

Stable snaps → near 1 Wild fluctuation → lower score

9️⃣ Ceiling Score


ceiling_score =
max(last_5_games_pts)
÷ positional_max_last_5

Fully numeric.

🔟 Replacement Delta


replacement_delta =
(player_projection - next_best_option_projection)
÷ positional_average_projection

This is decision-relative.

CONFIDENCE ENGINE v1.0
Confidence should answer:
“How safe is this decision relative to alternatives?”
It is NOT:
	•	How good the player is
	•	How high the projection is
	•	How much you like him
It is:
Score Separation adjusted for Risk

Step 1: Core Variables
For a start/sit decision between Player A and Player B:
We already have:


score_A
score_B
volatility_A
volatility_B
role_risk_A
role_risk_B

We compute:


score_gap = score_A - score_B

Absolute gap determines base certainty.

Step 2: Base Confidence (Gap Driven)
We convert gap into a percentage.
Use this structure:


base_confidence = min(95, 50 + (score_gap * 5))

Meaning:
Gap = 0 → 50% Gap = 2 → 60% Gap = 4 → 70% Gap = 6 → 80% Gap = 9 → 95% (capped)
This makes intuitive sense.

Step 3: Risk Adjustment Layer
Now we adjust for volatility and role instability.
First compute combined risk:


combined_volatility = (volatility_A + volatility_B) / 2
combined_role_risk = (role_risk_A + role_risk_B) / 2

Now apply penalty:


risk_penalty = combined_volatility * 10
role_penalty = (1 - combined_role_risk) * 10

Then:


confidence = base_confidence - risk_penalty - role_penalty

Clamp between 50–95%.

Step 4: Interpretation Bands
Confidence
Meaning

75–89%

60–74%

52–59%

50–51%
Coin Flip
Below 50% shouldn’t happen — that means reversed ranking.

Step 5: Why This Works
Confidence increases when:
	•	Score gap increases
	•	Volatility decreases
	•	Role stability increases
Confidence decreases when:
	•	Players are similar
	•	Boom/bust players involved
	•	Role uncertain
This mirrors real fantasy thinking.

🔥 Example Scenario
Player A = 82 Player B = 75
Gap = 7
Base confidence:


50 + (7 × 5) = 85%

Volatility avg = 0.4 Role stability avg = 0.9
Risk penalty:


0.4 × 10 = 4
(1 - 0.9) × 10 = 1

Final confidence:


85 - 4 - 1 = 80%

Output: Clear Lean (80%)
Feels real.
